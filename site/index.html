      How the ratings work

      Each stat compares you with the rest of the league. CAP: average captain points. TRF: points gained from transfers (players in minus players out, the week they moved). BEN: fewest points left on the bench. CON: steadiest weekly scores. CLU: weeks finishing in the league's top three. CHP: how much better you score in chip weeks. Overall mixes the six with your league position. Card colour: gold 80+, silver 70+, bronze below that, and In Form for a top-three average over the last three weeks.
